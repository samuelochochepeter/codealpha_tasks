# Content provenance

## Vocabulary dataset
Wikivoyage contributors, Idoma phrasebook, revision 5190968.
https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Idoma_phrasebook&oldid=5190968
Retrieved 1 October 2026. CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The first 20 lesson pairs use the source's written spelling. Lesson titles, grouping, learning tips, and quiz generation were added. The dataset and adaptations remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Their inclusion is not a claim of native-speaker verification. Community review pending.

## User-supplied learning resource
How to Speak Idoma Language: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oUxT8va0FYI
Title found in search results; audio and transcript unavailable during development. External link only. No downloaded clips, copied captions, or recordings are bundled.

## Additional listening resource
Assumpta Umele, Greetings and their responses in Idoma language, 24 October 2023.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nsEERZQVrMg
External link only. Its description uses a more detailed tone-marked spelling. It was consulted as a cross-check for greetings, not copied as an app transcript. A fluent teacher should reconcile spelling and dialect differences.

## Added beginner courses (1 October 2026)
Wikivoyage contributors:
- French phrasebook: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/French_phrasebook
- Spanish phrasebook: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Spanish_phrasebook
- Chinese (Mandarin) phrasebook: https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mandarin_phrasebook

CC BY-SA 4.0. Starter expressions and counting vocabulary were checked against these resources. Punctuation, case, English labels, lesson grouping, and learning tips were adapted. Mandarin uses simplified characters and tone-marked Pinyin, including neutral syllables. This is a small beginner course, not a complete language curriculum. None of these courses bundles audio or claims to assess spoken pronunciation.
